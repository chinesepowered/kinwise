Who it's for
The adult children who look after a parent from another city, and the parents who want to stay independent without handing over their accounts.
How it works

Quiet by default. An agent only when money is at risk.

1 · Quiet mode · no model

Deterministic sweep

10 scam detectors run on every charge, call, text and email. A correlator scores signals in a 3-hour window and wakes the agent only at score ≥ 4 with money at risk.

New money linked to an open incident is held instantly by policy.
